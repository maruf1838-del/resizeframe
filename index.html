How to resize a photo for social media

  1. Upload your photo by clicking the box or dropping a file into it.
  2. Choose the platform and image type, such as Instagram profile picture or YouTube banner.
  3. Drag the preview to position the part of the photo you want. Use Zoom to crop closer.
  4. Pick JPG, PNG or WebP, then select Download image.

Why the right size matters

Uploading a photo with the wrong proportions makes platforms crop it automatically, often cutting off faces or text. Resizing first lets you choose the crop yourself, and keeps your image sharp because it is not stretched or recompressed more than once. Round profile pictures are cropped from a square, so keep the subject centered.

Frequently asked questions

Is my photo uploaded to a server?

No. All resizing happens in your browser using your device. Your photos are never sent to us.

Is the tool free?

Yes. It is free with no watermark and no account. The site is supported by advertising.

Which format should I choose?

JPG gives small files for photos. PNG keeps sharp text and logos. WebP is smaller than both and supported by most platforms.

Why is my profile picture shown as a circle?

Most platforms display profile pictures as circles but require a square upload. The preview shows the circular view so you can check the framing.
